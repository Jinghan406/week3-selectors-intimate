Advanced InterfaceWeek 3 — SelectorsSet 03Tone: Intimate

Ink line

Controls drawn with a pen. They ink on, erase backward, and wobble when you hover.

Switch

A pencil capsule. Turning on drags the knob across and hatches the track in indigo behind it.

    Checkbox

    The check is one quick pen stroke that runs past the box. Unchecking erases it backward.

      Radio

      Picking one circles it with a pen loop and scribbles the dot in. The old loop erases.

        Spec

        px at 16 px = 1 em. Jitter is in control units.
